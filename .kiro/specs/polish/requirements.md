# Requirements: polish (wave 3)
1. WHEN any page loads, THE shared theme SHALL render a night background with a fog or moonlight gradient and one accent colour (lantern amber).
2. THE "Needs you" cards SHALL be tombstone-shaped and still show the level text.
3. WHILE `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is set, THE theme SHALL run no animation.
4. THE theme SHALL keep every text/background pair at WCAG AA (4.5:1 body, 3:1 non-text) or better, checked by a test.
5. THE landing page SHALL show a one-line pitch, an inline SVG "1 skeleton, 2 apps" diagram, links to both apps and to `https://github.com/thiagopari/graveyard-shift`.
6. EACH app SHALL link back to the landing page.
7. BEFORE the first photo, Mussel Memory SHALL show an upload prompt and a "Try a sample" button using bundled synthetic images.
8. WHEN Page Up or Page Down is pressed on the Bridge Troll slider, THE app SHALL move the replay by 6 hours.
9. BOTH Markdown exports SHALL defer `URL.revokeObjectURL`.
10. Bridge Troll SHALL show a "Data gaps" note for the Essex Jct gap and East Georgia's missing major threshold.
