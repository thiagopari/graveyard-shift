# Product: Graveyard Shift

Graveyard Shift is a Kiroween 2026 Skeleton Crew entry. One small "skeleton" library (4 bones) powers several night-shift triage apps.

The four bones: Evidence, Checks, Triage, Inbox.

- Every decision shown to a human cites its evidence (id, source, time, license).
- Triage levels are exactly: Needs you, Watch, Fine.
- A reading too poor to trust is never given a score. The app asks for a retake.
- Public or synthetic data only. No real-site images.
- Apps: `mussel-memory` (water-intake screen triage from photos), `bridge-troll` (wave 2, Vermont July 2023 flood replay).
