Bridge Troll

Replay of the July 10-11, 2023 Vermont flood. Each Winooski and Lamoille River bridge is triaged from the nearest USGS gauge against NWS flood categories, plus its NBI scour code and substructure rating. Everything runs in your browser from public data.

Use the arrow keys to move 15 minutes, Page Up or Page Down for 6 hours. Readings are the latest observation at or before this time, never interpolated.
