▲ Graveyard Shift

One small skeleton library triages whatever needs a night watch into Needs you, Watch or Fine.

Every decision cites its evidence. A reading too poor to trust is never scored; the app asks for a retake.

The four bones

  1. Evidence: every decision cites an id, source, time and licence.
  2. Checks: quality gates. A failed blocking check means no score.
  3. Triage: risk and checks become Needs you, Watch or Fine.
  4. Inbox: accessible cards, sorted worst first, with a Markdown export.

Two apps

  • Mussel Memory

    Water-intake screen triage from a photo. Blur, exposure, haze and framing are checked first, then percent blocked drives the level. Runs in your browser; photos are never uploaded.

  • Bridge Troll

    Replay of the July 10-11, 2023 Vermont flood. A time slider moves USGS gauge readings against NWS flood categories and ranks Winooski and Lamoille River bridges from the FHWA National Bridge Inventory.
